# International investigation design

Registry owns a planned, non-executable contract for selective cooperation between agencies. Desktop is a local document/evidence workspace in that design, not an official international transport or an agency-qualified client.

Canonical contract: [Registry 0.1.0](https://github.com/mithril-lang/mithril-registry/blob/3c65bd553f8cae684a87863ed8c37bb4d8a19af5/solutions/international-investigation.json); [full design](https://github.com/mithril-lang/mithril-registry/blob/3c65bd553f8cae684a87863ed8c37bb4d8a19af5/docs/security-integrations/international-investigation.md). Fund pins the same bytes separately from its agency catalog. See [[lat.md/desktop-security#Runtime security]] and [[lat.md/cloud-workspace#Cloud workspace]].

## Local evidence and authority

Each agency keeps its domestic case, source custody and authority. A collaboration ID links authorized references; it does not grant other agencies access to local files, Hermes profiles or account-owned cloud records.

Existing Registry evaluation components can pack retained source bytes, create signed manifests/custody records and verify against independent external trust. They are local CLI/stdio tools, not a new Desktop forensic screen or remote MCP. Tool availability, native profile selection, device login, bearer scope and a signer key do not establish institutional identity or cross-border authority.

## Shared materials and review

The proposed journey prepares selected materials, reviews the recipient, purpose, version and conditions, and records handoff and receipt separately. Dispatch and case synchronization remain unimplemented.

Proposal fields bind sender/recipient, legal basis and authority references, selected package/manifest digest, classification, jurisdictions, storage, retention/onward-sharing conditions, expiry and channel. Changes invalidate approval. Original and redacted/translated outputs have separate versions and provenance. Translation records source digest, language, method, reviewer and gaps. Confidential police/prosecution/counsel/defense/judicial records remain separated.

## Transport and uncertain outcomes

Competent authorities select an approved external or offline channel per case. Desktop must distinguish dispatch, receipt, integrity, receiving authority, completeness and judicial decisions, keeping partial and unknown outcomes visible.

No INTERPOL/SIENA connector, mutual-assistance submission, agency identity federation, remote device acquisition or global case database is added. Unknown send outcomes require operation/official receipt reconciliation before retry. Revocation stops future access/dispatch and records recipient notification; it cannot promise remote deletion of already exported bytes or automatically release retention holds.

## Consumer boundaries and acceptance

Web shares canonical UI but has no native local-file/shell authority. Mobile currently remains the chat/balance/device-grant companion. Cross-device case actions require separate scoped APIs, institutional acceptance and client validation.

Begin with two synthetic agencies, separate trust and offline handoff. Evaluate wrong recipient, altered package, expired authority, version invalidation, translation traceability, party isolation and uncertain/partial results. Only after jurisdiction, channel, deployment, storage and operational owners are confirmed can a limited institutional evaluation proceed. This prose-only integration changes no packaged app behavior or installer.
